# Retention Playbook

## Price-sensitive customers
Customers who mention price increases or cheaper competitors respond best to a loyalty discount
of 10-20% for 6 months or a downgrade offer to a lower plan instead of cancelling.
Never offer more than 20% without manager approval. Highlight the ROI of premium features they already use.

## Low product engagement
Customers with low weekly logins and low feature adoption need onboarding help.
Offer a free 30-minute training session, share a personalised "quick wins" checklist,
and assign a customer success manager for accounts on standard or premium plans.

## Service quality and outages
After repeated outages or slow support, escalate to the engineering on-call lead,
send a personal apology from the account owner, and offer a service credit of one month.
Follow up within 48 hours with a post-incident summary.

## Billing problems
Double charges or late-payment disputes must be refunded within 3 business days.
Offer flexible payment dates or annual billing with a discount to reduce payment friction.
Escalate unresolved billing tickets to the finance team the same day.

## Long-tenure loyal customers
Customers with tenure above 36 months should receive a thank-you gesture,
early access to new features, and an invitation to the customer advisory board.

## Do not
Do not promise features that are not on the roadmap. Do not contact customers more than
twice per week. Always respect opt-out preferences.
